Why Quantum-Behaved Optimization Matters

The Classical PSO Stagnation Trap

In classical PSO, particle velocities are deterministically bounded. Once the swarm coalesces around a sub-optimal basin of attraction, momentum decays and the swarm stagnates, leading to expensive routes with 5–15% excess mileage.

Quantum Delta-Potential Tunneling

QPSO models particles governed by the Schrödinger wave equation inside an attractive delta-potential well. Every particle maintains a non-zero probability of tunneling to any coordinates in $\mathbb{R}^n$, escaping local minima and converging within 0.5% of optimal BKS.

Core Algorithmic Architecture

Phase 1
Wavefunction Sampling

Particles update positions using Gaussian stochastic attractors and mean-best ($mbest$) contraction-expansion scaling.

Phase 2
LOV Mapping

Largest Order Value maps continuous quantum state vectors into valid discrete customer delivery permutations.

Phase 3
Prins Split DAG

Computes the globally optimal partition of customer sequences into feasible vehicle tours in $\mathcal{O}(n^2)$ time.

Phase 4
2-Opt Polish

Iterative intra-route edge swap heuristic untangles crossings to achieve mathematical optimality.
