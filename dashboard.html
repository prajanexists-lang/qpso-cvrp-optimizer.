QPSO CVRP Mathematical Formulation

1. Capacitated Vehicle Routing Problem (CVRP)

Given a graph $G=(V, E)$ with depot $v_0$ and customers $V \setminus \{v_0\}$, demands $q_i$, vehicle capacity $Q$, find $K$ minimum-cost tours departing and returning to $v_0$:

$$\min \sum_{k=1}^K \sum_{(i,j) \in R_k} c_{ij}, \quad ext{s.t.} \sum_{i \in R_k} q_i \le Q \quad orall k \in \{1,\dots,K\}$$

2. Quantum Delta-Potential Well (Sun et al. 2004)

Particles follow Schrödinger wave equation with delta-potential well centered at attractor $p_i$:

$$p_{i,d}(t) = \phi \cdot P_{i,d}(t) + (1-\phi) \cdot G_d(t), \quad \phi \sim U(0,1)$$ $$X_{i,d}(t+1) = p_{i,d}(t) \pm lpha \cdot |mbest_d(t) - X_{i,d}(t)| \cdot \ln\left( rac{1}{u} ight), \quad u \sim U(0, 1)$$

Where Mean Best position is $mbest(t) = rac{1}{M}\sum_{i=1}^M P_i(t)$ and $lpha$ linearly decays from $lpha_{\max} o lpha_{\min}$.

3. Prins (2004) Optimal Split Decoder

Continuous swarm position vectors are sorted using Largest Order Value (LOV) to produce a giant tour permutation $\pi$. An auxiliary DAG is constructed where edge $(i, j)$ represents a valid feasible route from depot, serving $\pi_{i+1} \dots \pi_j$, with weight equal to route distance. Dijkstra / Bellman-Ford computes globally optimal fleet routes in $\mathcal{O}(n^2)$ time.
